DNA Extraction: From Tissue to Analysis-Ready DNA

A conceptual guide to obtaining high-quality DNA for molecular and genomic analyses.

Why DNA quality matters

Downstream PCR, library preparation, sequencing, and variant discovery depend on DNA concentration, purity, integrity, and consistency across samples.

Quality checks

Concentration may be assessed with fluorometric or spectrophotometric methods, purity with absorbance ratios, and integrity with gel electrophoresis where appropriate.

Common causes of poor extraction

  • Too much starting tissue
  • Incomplete lysis or digestion
  • Carry-over of salts, ethanol, or proteins
  • Repeated freeze–thaw cycles
  • Cross-contamination during handling
Practice note: Always follow the current manufacturer instructions, institutional biosafety requirements, sample permits, and laboratory supervision.
