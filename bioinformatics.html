Tools in my workflow

Depending on the project and biological question, my work uses tools including STACKS, PLINK, VCFtools, ADMIXTURE, STRUCTURE, Arlequin, adegenet, dartR, NextGenMap, R, Bash, Linux, and HPC environments.

Quality control before biological storytelling

  • Confirm sample names and population maps
  • Assess missing data and genotype quality
  • Check sequencing batches and read-length differences
  • Evaluate relatedness and linkage disequilibrium
  • Perform sensitivity analyses under alternative filters
  • Compare results across complementary methods

PCA, ADMIXTURE, DAPC, and FST answer related but different questions. Agreement strengthens interpretation; disagreement should trigger investigation, not selective reporting.
