About

Research at the intersection of genomics, ecology, and environmental health.

I am a PhD candidate in Cellular and Molecular Biology at the United Arab Emirates University. My doctoral research uses genome-wide SNP data and population-genomic analyses to understand the diversity, connectivity, differentiation, and evolutionary dynamics of Arabian Gulf marine fishes.

My work spans the complete research process—from biological sampling and molecular workflows to high-performance computing, variant filtering, statistical analysis, visualisation, and biological interpretation. I also have experience in environmental toxicology and trace-element research in marine organisms.

Selected projects

Doctoral research

Population genomics of Arabian Gulf marine fishes

End-to-end ddRAD-seq research across two marine fish species, including read processing, reference alignment, SNP discovery, filtering, population structure, genetic diversity, differentiation, connectivity, and effective population-size analyses.

Computational genomics

Reproducible bioinformatics pipelines

Development and troubleshooting of Linux-, R-, Bash-, and HPC-based workflows for large-scale genomic datasets, quality assurance, statistical analysis, and publication-ready visualisation.

Environmental science

Trace elements in marine organisms

Assessment of essential and potentially toxic elements to examine environmental exposure, physiological regulation, ecological relevance, and possible seafood-safety implications.
