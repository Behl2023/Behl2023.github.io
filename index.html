About

Research at the intersection of genes, organisms, and changing environments.

I am a PhD candidate in Cellular and Molecular Science in the Department of Biology at United Arab Emirates University, Al Ain. My work combines molecular ecology, conservation genomics, population genetics, marine biodiversity, bioinformatics, and environmental toxicology.

My academic journey spans forensic science, toxicology, antimicrobial technologies, marine fieldwork, DNA-based research, and population-genomic analysis.
