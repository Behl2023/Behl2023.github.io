Research

Research at the interface of genes, organisms, and changing marine environments

My work links molecular data with ecological and conservation questions while maintaining rigorous quality control and reproducible analysis.

Population Genomics

Genome-wide SNP data can reveal variation within populations, differentiation among regions, gene flow, population structure, and demographic patterns. My work uses complementary methods rather than relying on a single plot or statistic.

Conservation Genomics

Genomic evidence can improve biodiversity assessment, identify management units, evaluate connectivity, and support sustainable fisheries and conservation decisions.

Environmental Toxicology

I also investigate essential and potentially toxic elements in marine organisms using methods such as ICP-MS and AAS, with attention to ecological exposure and seafood-safety context.

Reproducible Bioinformatics

I develop and troubleshoot workflows using Linux, Bash, R, HPC systems, variant filtering, population maps, batch-effect diagnostics, and publication-ready visualisation.

Current research status

Detailed genomic findings from my current marine-fish projects are being prepared for scientific publication. This website presents the study context, methods, research questions, and publicly shareable information without disclosing unpublished results.
